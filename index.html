Find trackers and surveillance devices around you

Last updated 2026-08-30

GhostTooth listens to the Bluetooth advertisements around you and flags what looks like a tracker or recording hardware. Free, no ads, no account, and nothing ever leaves your phone.

Get it on Google PlayBrowser scanner (archived)

What it detects

  • Trackers: Apple AirTag and Find My accessories, Tile, Samsung SmartTag, Chipolo, Pebblebee, Moto Tag and Google Find My Device network tags.
  • Surveillance devices: camera glasses (Ray-Ban Meta, Snap Spectacles, Echo Frames), wearable recorders and AI pins, body cameras.
  • Anonymous beacons that stay with you across several distinct time windows, even without a recognisable brand.

Every verdict explains itself

A red badge without an explanation helps nobody. GhostTooth tells you why a device was flagged and how strong the evidence is: possible (a single weak hint), likely (corroborated by the maker or the advertised service) or confirmed (read straight from the tracking protocol). Only strong evidence raises an alert.

Background monitoring

Switch monitoring on and GhostTooth keeps looking while the app is closed, with a notification carrying live counts. It tells you when a device is travelling with you — the signal that actually matters.

Privacy

No scan data, no personal data and no location ever leaves your device. No ads, no trackers, no account. The only network connection fetches detection definitions from this site. See the privacy policy.
